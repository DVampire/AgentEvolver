Generate a workflow called `triage_workflow` that classifies an incoming report and routes it to the handling that classification implies. - One step reads the report and decides a severity: `critical`, `normal`, or `noise`. - `critical` goes to a step that writes an escalation summary; `normal` goes to a step that writes a short acknowledgement; `noise` ends the run without further work. - The run's outcome names the severity that was chosen and what was written.
