Community learning finder

Create a trustworthy website that helps residents discover and register interest in affordable local classes, workshops, and clubs.

Core user journey

Visitors should be able to browse realistic offerings, narrow them by useful criteria, understand key details, select an offering, and complete a lightweight registration-interest flow with a clear confirmation. No real payment or account is required.

Product expectations

  • Use realistic, internally consistent content rather than lorem ipsum.
  • Make schedule, venue, price, availability, prerequisites, and accessibility information easy to understand where relevant.
  • Support mobile and desktop layouts, keyboard use, clear validation, and recovery from invalid or empty states.
  • Provide the shared in-product feedback and receipt flow required by the demo task.

Creative latitude

Choose the visual identity, information architecture, technical stack, and exact content. Prefer a coherent, polished small product over a broad but unfinished portal.
