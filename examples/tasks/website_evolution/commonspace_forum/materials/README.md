# COMMONSPACE sample materials

These original, fictional materials are supplied for building and trying the product. They are not factual engineering advice or real community activity. They may be imported, displayed and adapted within the demo. Preserve the source title, revision and fictional status when presenting them.

| Project | Visual material | Notes | Observations | Later revision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Narrow growing space | [Growing-space sketch](growing-space.svg) | [Grower's notes](growing-notes.md) | [Space observations](growing-observations.csv) | [Revised observations](growing-observations-v2.csv) |
| Shared repair bench | [Bench sketch](repair-bench.svg) | [Workshop notes](repair-notes.md) | [Trial sessions](repair-observations.csv) | Create a labelled trial revision during the experience check. |

The two sketches have different aspect ratios. Their shapes and labels give people meaningful places to refer to. The CSV rows have stable observation IDs, but a later revision changes a value and its confidence. Notes contain competing preferences, not an agreed answer. The material is deliberately small enough to inspect completely.

Create another project or change a sample through the same supported import/authoring controls to check that the experience works beyond these examples. Keep generated trial content labelled. The builder chooses the presentation and interaction; this folder does not prescribe a parser, layout or software component.
