# A shared bench for a small repair circle

Source: COMMONSPACE fictional demo material. Revision 1.

Rowan: “When we gather around a broken stool, small parts end up in the working area. I would like a removable tray near the back of the bench, with a place to leave a note about what each part is.”

Ash: “I usually work from the lower edge in the sketch. A deep tray at the back may be awkward for me to reach. Can we compare a side tray and a shallow strip without deciding for everyone?”

Rowan: “I care about keeping the centre clear. Ash cares about reach. We both want to pack everything away at the end. Reusing spare offcuts sounds attractive, but we have not agreed on dimensions.”

The trials are fictional informal observations, not ergonomic or safety guidance. There is no proven best layout. Useful proposals should preserve who raised each concern and make it easy to question a cited trial, point to a position or suggest a different arrangement.
