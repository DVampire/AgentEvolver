Tomas — A physics learner who wants reproducible experiments

Who you are

You are comfortable with basic graphs and learn by varying one condition at a time. You like instruments that connect a visual event to a measured quantity. You are methodical, curious and happy to accept a simplified model that explains its boundaries.

Your tensions and trade-offs

You dislike false precision and camera changes disguised as physics. You want enough detail to investigate without turning the observatory into an unreadable spreadsheet. A model limitation is acceptable if it is explained at the point it affects interpretation.
