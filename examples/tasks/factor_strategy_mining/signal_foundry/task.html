Project brief · Research workbench · From factors to strategies

Signal Foundry

Turn a stock's price and volume history into explainable factors, testable trading hypotheses and a beautiful research workbench whose conclusions can be inspected.

Mission and research scope

Create an interactive workbench for researching a stock, discovering useful factors and turning them into testable trading strategies. The two research stages should form one continuous report page, with clear conclusions and evidence that readers can explore by scrolling, without switching pages or tabs between the stages.

The accompanying study.json specifies the stock, dates, trading assumptions, research budget and numerical acceptance targets. The default study uses NVDA daily OHLCV. Detailed implementation choices remain open.

The user must be able to answer: What did we download? Why might this factor work? Which evidence admitted or rejected it? How did it change a strategy? What survives costs, another market regime and the untouched final evaluation?

A trustworthy data foundation

Base the research on real, lawfully accessible stock prices and trading volume, with the corporate actions and trading-session information needed to interpret them. Readers should be able to identify the source, actual coverage and adjustment assumptions, and understand any missing data or access limitation. Support research on another stock or date range through the same product.

Retain raw prices and volume, split and dividend records, provider/feed identity, retrieval time, dataset hashes and a data-quality report. Detect duplicate bars, missing sessions, invalid OHLC ranges and inconsistent adjustments. A single-venue volume feed, fabricated data or a short convenience sample cannot silently replace the requested study.

Use lawfully accessible public historical data to complete the research. If verified as-traded prices or consolidated volume are unavailable, the study permits a documented adjusted-price research proxy. Preserve provider fields, adjustment rules and limitations. Show strict data qualification separately as unmet where necessary; this does not block factor evaluation, strategy research or numerical reports on the declared public-data basis. Never present proxy fills as verified historical executions or change the statistical targets.

Distinguish measured data quality from assumptions. Unavailable history must remain visible as a limitation rather than being replaced by fabricated observations.

Stage 1 — Factor discovery and the Factor Observatory

Discover interpretable signals, compare their predictive usefulness and select a complementary factor library for strategy research. Each conclusion must come from reproducible calculations on the specified observations.

Explore distinct economic hypotheses such as trend persistence, reversal after shocks, volume-confirmed moves, range structure, volatility compression and regime transitions. Give every candidate an ID, formula, units, lookback, availability time, prediction horizon, direction, rationale and falsification condition. Parameter variations count as trials. Select directions and transformations on training observations only.

Show every proposed and evaluated factor in the report's factor section, distinguishing pending, blocked and failed evaluations from admitted or rejected research candidates. Readers must see which factors were mined, their formulas and how well each performed. Include a sortable candidate table, time-series IC and RankIC by horizon and fold, rolling stability, quantile response curves, coverage and missingness, uncertainty, regime behavior, redundancy clusters and admission reasons. Define metrics before use. A single stock has no daily cross-sectional IC. Show insufficient evidence explicitly instead of inventing confidence or passing a factor.

The factor library is the contract for stage two. Preserve exact expressions, fitted state, code/data versions and admission evidence. Factors rejected on validation cannot reappear as eligible simply because a strategy needs them.

Stage 2 — Strategy mining and the Strategy Atelier

Turn selected factors into executable trading hypotheses and evaluate their returns, risks and costs. Readers should be able to trace a strategy back to its factor evidence and understand how signals lead to positions, trades and profit or loss.

Design interpretable single-factor baselines before combining complementary factors. Specify entry, exit, sizing, rebalance timing, neutral states and regime rules. Use the declared long-or-cash execution model. Measure the incremental value of each factor with ablations and compare against cash and holding the same stock. Rank candidates using the frozen validation objective, risk and complexity, not the prettiest training curve.

List all strategy candidates with their exact factor composition, readable trading rules, actual evaluation state, results and selection/rejection reasons. Show labelled train, validation and final-test sections together on the same page, with gross/net equity, benchmark comparison, drawdown and recovery, monthly/yearly returns, exposure, turnover, transaction costs, trade distributions and traceable orders. Report CAGR, annualized volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, maximum drawdown, trade count, win rate, payoff/profit factor and cost sensitivity with definitions and null states. Include fold/regime results, parameter stability, uncertainty, factor ablations and all unsuccessful candidates.

Research loop and the final evaluation

Iterate factor and strategy research on train and validation: diagnose a specific weakness, investigate a new hypothesis and record the outcome. Each iteration explains the evidence, the intended improvement and whether measured results improved afterward. Carry strategy diagnoses back into factor research when useful. Keep a complete trial history including rejected candidates, validation exposure, cost and changes in reasoning.

Keep training, validation and test results distinct. Selection must use only information available at the relevant time, with no future-data leakage. Fix the evaluation criteria before research and finalize the selected factors and strategy before the final test. Both report sections should describe that same final evaluation; test factor rankings must not influence a strategy that is still being optimized.

Continue the connected factor and strategy investigation while the objective remains unmet and useful research is possible within the declared budget. Preliminary eligibility is not sufficient to submit a final strategy: first meet the study's submission requirements on validation data, demonstrate robustness and justify closing the search. Publishing a report or completing a batch does not complete the research objective.

Successful research requires every frozen final-test strategy criterion to pass on real data. A failed test remains a failed attempt, with the overall objective unmet. Continue useful research on the permitted research data; new confirmation requires unused evaluation data under a protocol fixed before access. Repeatedly tuning against the same test invalidates a confirmatory claim. If budgets or the declared no-improvement limit are exhausted, or a concrete prerequisite leaves no useful work possible, preserve the unsuccessful outcome, remaining requirements and the conditions for resuming. A new folder, seed or tighter slice does not make seen data unseen; further confirmatory research needs a genuinely new evaluation period. State how the evaluation was controlled and distinguish self-review from independent evidence.

A research workbench worth exploring

Design a premium analytical interface: disciplined typography, calm surfaces, deliberate spacing, deep-green backgrounds, layered green surfaces, mint emphasis, light text, amber uncertainty and red losses. Use a coherent visual direction and an investigative layout rather than interchangeable KPI cards.

Use one continuous page for the overview, factor inventory and evidence, strategy inventory and evidence, research decisions and metric definitions. A table of contents may scroll to sections; it must not replace them with another page or a hidden tab. Local sorting, filtering and chart comparisons should preserve the surrounding investigation. All factor and strategy summary rows should be available by default.

  • A study overview connects source provenance, the chronological split timeline, research progress and separate engineering/research acceptance states.
  • A Factor Observatory lets a visitor filter candidates, compare horizons and folds, inspect a formula and trace selection into a strategy.
  • A Strategy Atelier links factor composition, ablations, equity/drawdown charts, trade markers, fold comparisons and a searchable trade ledger.
  • A synchronized time brush and chart tooltips explain which trades and exposures contributed to a selected drawdown. Descriptive filtering must not become test optimization.
  • A cost-sensitivity explorer uses predeclared scenarios computed from the frozen backtest. Mark exploratory controls clearly; they cannot overwrite the accepted result.
  • An experiment lineage view links hypotheses, rejected trials, versions and artifacts. Visitors can download the permitted metric tables, a compact structured analysis summary and a reproducibility manifest.
  • Every line or bar chart states its question, axes, units, series, time range, aggregation and sample support. Show baselines, uncertainty where justified, missing values and a readable conclusion. Definitions, charts, tables and downloads must agree.
  • The final-test panel stays unavailable until the final evaluation. Test values must not be accessible through other report views or downloads beforehand.

Provide real keyboard interactions, readable charts, responsive layouts and useful loading, empty and failed states. The main investigation should work on desktop and narrow screens. Publish measured factor research first, then extend that same page with strategy evidence in a later release. Keep unfinished sections clearly pending, with working links and evidence that the key reader journeys function as intended.

Delivery and acceptance evidence

  • A working data-to-factor-to-strategy research workflow that can evaluate changed inputs and reproduce its conclusions.
  • Real data provenance, frozen protocol, factor library, strategy specifications, trial history, deterministic checks and reproducible machine-readable metrics.
  • One detailed, continuous report page showing which factors and strategies were explored, what their definitions are and how they performed, including rejected candidates. Both stages remain readable without changing pages or tabs. Displayed metrics and chart values reconcile with exported records, with usable scroll navigation and interactions.
  • Explicit final outcome: passed, failed, inconclusive or blocked; each unmet numerical criterion, test exposure state, limitations and a precise reproduction/resume path.

This is historical research software. Do not place orders, promise future profit, purchase data subscriptions or claim independent evaluation from self-review.
